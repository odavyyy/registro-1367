Terms of Use (summary)

  • Free fan game: no accounts, no purchases, no ads, no donations. In-game coins, skins and items have no money value.
  • Allowed: playing for personal use, and making videos, streams and screenshots (including on monetized channels) if you credit the game with a link and don't claim it as yours.
  • Not allowed: re-uploading, selling or redistributing the game or its characters; attacking itch.io, GitHub or the authors' accounts; spam, harassment, hate speech or sharing other people's personal data in comments.
  • Alpha: the game may have bugs, change, lose saves or go offline.
  • Health: jumpscares, flashing lights and loud sounds. If you have photosensitive epilepsy, consult a doctor first. Recommended 12+ (the authors' recommendation, not an official rating).
  • Passarex, Clubex and the characters belong to the group Os Bacanos. Third-party materials are listed in the licenses table.

Full version (PT): Termos de Uso.

Privacy (summary)

  • We don't ask for any personal data and we have no server or database of our own.
  • Your progress is saved only in your browser (localStorage). You can delete it in Options › Delete my data for this game.
  • GitHub Pages (this site, where the game also runs) and itch.io (if you play there) receive technical access data such as your IP address. itch.io shows us only aggregate statistics.
  • The download version stores the game files and your progress on your device so it works offline; none of it is sent to us. Remove it by deleting the app or the site data in your browser.
  • The YouTube trailer loads only when you click it, and the Suno music player only when you open the in-game radio.
  • You have rights under Brazil's LGPD and your local law. Contact us via Instagram DM @odavyyy1.
