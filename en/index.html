What is it

Incident 1367 (in Portuguese, Registro 1367) is a fan game set in the universe of Os Bacanos, a Brazilian comedy-horror series. You watch an old tape from the “Birdie Channel” and get pulled inside it, where the Passarex is waiting: a man-sized bird with a human head that stands still in the dark and whistles, “fiu fiu”.

Inside the tape: dream hills with a little pink house, endless yellow rooms, a playroom with eight doors, a ship in 1491, the streets of Naviraí, a Cuphead-style boss fight against the Passarex with guavas, and a party at the Gates of Clubex at the end. There are 3 endings, and the game keeps count of how many times he noticed you.

Language

Pick English on the start screen. The voices stay in the original Brazilian Portuguese (that’s the soul of it), with English subtitles for every line, sign, note and sound.
